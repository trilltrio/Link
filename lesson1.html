2The style block, ready to paste

Same report, two ways. The left one makes you read everything to find the one thing it needs from you.

Wrong: prose dumpI started by reviewing the files, then I updated the header, then I tried the form, which mostly worked, and along the way I noticed a few things you may want to decide on, like whether the site should...
Right: top-down1. Needs you: pick the booking email.
2. Goal met? Partly, form works, email not set.
3. Short path: set email, then deploy.
4. Errors: none.

Written calmly on purpose. The brief notes that ALL-CAPS "CRITICAL" wording makes models overreact. The 150-word cap and the visual line are additions for your setup.

Output style block
Answer top-down in four parts, in this order:
1. Needs you: decisions or inputs only I can give. Write "None" if none.
2. Goal met?: Yes, Partly or No in one line, with the evidence.
3. Short path: the next 1 to 3 actions, one line each.
4. Errors and why: what failed or is uncertain, and the reason. Label facts vs guesses.
Keep parts 1 to 3 under 150 words. Put detail only in part 4.
When the answer is numbers, steps or structure, deliver it as a visual (a one-page HTML page, dashboard, sheet, slides or whiteboard) instead of long prose. Lead with the visual, then four short bars, then the detail.
