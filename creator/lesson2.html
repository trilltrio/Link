1The full job, not a step

The video says top users hand over the whole outcome. A step makes the agent wait for you after every move; the whole job lets it plan the order itself.

Wrong: a stepMake a header for the site.
Right: the full jobBuild the whole site that gets customers to order.

5Can't fill the four? Get interviewed

When the job is still fuzzy, the video's move is to flip it: the agent asks, you answer, and it writes the brief.

Interview prompt
Before doing anything, interview me so you can write a full brief for this job. Ask me questions until you can cover the full job, why it matters, the guardrails, and what done looks like with checks you can run yourself. Then show me the brief in four parts and wait for my go.
